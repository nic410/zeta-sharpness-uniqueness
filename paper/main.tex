\begin{abstract}
Weil's explicit formula for $\zeta(s)$ equates a functional $\Arch(F)$, which depends only on the archimedean data of $\zeta$, with sums over
the zeros and over the prime powers. We construct an explicit even function $F=\Xi^2H$, with $H$ entire and positive on $\RR$, such that
$F\ge0$ and $\hF\ge0$ on $\RR$, $\hF$ vanishes at $\log n/2\pi$ for every integer $n\ge2$, and $\Arch(F)=0$. Since $\Xi$ vanishes at every
non-trivial zero, on or off the critical line, no information about the zeros is used. $F$ is an exact magic function, in the sense of the
sphere-packing bounds of Cohn--Elkies and Viazovska, for the linear programme of Odlyzko, Poitou and Serre: for the data of $\zeta$ the
programme has no positive slack, so the method is sharp, and $F$ is a minimiser under RH. With a support theorem of Part~I, every pair of
positive measures satisfying the formula, on the zero side and on the prime side beyond $\log2/2\pi$, is then $\zeta$'s own. This resolves
Conjectures~S and~U of Part~I. $H$ comes from an eigenvalue-$\frac14$ eigenfunction of the hyperbolic Laplacian through a contour argument of
Viazovska's type, with Green's identity in place of Cauchy's theorem; its positivity is partly computer-assisted. We do not prove RH; by
these results it is equivalent to the existence of such a pair.
\end{abstract}
